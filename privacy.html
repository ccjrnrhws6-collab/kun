Privacy Policy — AI Chat Exporter (CJK)

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Short version: your conversations never leave your browser. We don't collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

What the extension does

When you click the toolbar icon on a supported site (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, gemini.google.com, claude.ai, notebooklm.google.com), the extension reads the conversation that is currently shown on that page and converts it — locally, inside your browser — into a Markdown, PDF or Word file that you download to your own computer.

Data we handle

DataWhere it goesWhy
Conversation text on the open tabProcessed in memory in your browser only; never transmittedTo build the file you asked for
Export settings, free-trial counterchrome.storage.local on your deviceTo remember your preferences and remaining free exports
Pro license key (only if you enter one)Stored in chrome.storage.local; sent to our payment provider's license API (Lemon Squeezy, api.lemonsqueezy.com) to activate / periodically validate / deactivate it, together with a device label ("Chrome")To verify that you purchased Pro

That license check is the only network request the extension makes on its own, and it happens only after you enter a license key (activation), about once a week while Pro is active (re-validation), and when you click Deactivate. No conversation content, browsing history, or personal information is ever included.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or remote code.
  • No account or sign-in.
  • No access to websites other than the five listed above.
  • No selling or transferring of user data to third parties; no use of data for advertising, creditworthiness or any purpose unrelated to the single purpose of exporting your chat.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record). Their privacy policy applies to the checkout: https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. You can also click "Deactivate on this browser" to release the license seat first.

Contact

Contact: via the support link on the product's store page.

你在支援的網站（chatgpt.com、chat.openai.com、gemini.google.com、claude.ai、notebooklm.google.com）點擊工具列圖示時，擴充功能會讀取該分頁目前顯示的對話，在你的瀏覽器本機轉成 Markdown、PDF 或 Word 檔，下載到你的電腦。
